About the Project

The Lung Cancer Prediction System helps predict the likelihood of lung cancer using patient health information and a Deep Neural Network. By analyzing various clinical factors and lifestyle choices, the system aims to identify potential risks.

Please note that this system is designed for educational and demonstration purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis.

Our Mission

Early detection is crucial in the fight against lung cancer, as it significantly improves treatment outcomes and survival rates.

Our mission is to explore how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can assist healthcare professionals by providing data-driven insights and risk assessments, ultimately supporting the medical community in identifying at-risk individuals sooner.

Key Features

  • Patient prediction
  • Confidence score
  • Risk level classification
  • Prediction history
  • Dashboard analytics
  • Printable reports

How It Works

  1. User enters patient information: Submit clinical and lifestyle data via a simple form.
  2. The trained Deep Neural Network processes the data: The AI model scales and analyzes the input features.
  3. The model predicts the likelihood of lung cancer: A risk level and confidence score are generated.
  4. Results are stored in the database and displayed to the user: View reports and historical analytics seamlessly.
Medical Disclaimer

The predictions are generated by a machine learning model for educational purposes and should not replace medical advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
